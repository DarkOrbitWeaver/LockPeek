# Licensing decision — draft, not a license

The intended rules are free personal and business use, free modification and redistribution, no sale of LockPeek or modified versions, and a retained optional link to support the original creator. The exact definitions of resale, bundling, paid support, hosted services, attribution and downstream donation links need legal review. These notes do not grant rights and must not be published as an approved license.

A no-sale condition is source-available, not OSI open source. PolyForm Noncommercial may restrict ordinary business use. Commons Clause is narrower than a blanket non-commercial restriction but can affect paid support and integration scenarios; verify that its actual wording meets the requested policy before choosing it. Do not apply Creative Commons noncommercial terms to software merely as a shortcut.

Third-party dependencies and Ko-fi branding retain their own terms. Contributors must have authority to submit their changes under any chosen project terms.

References: https://commonsclause.com/ ; https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0 ; https://opensource.org/faq ; https://creativecommons.org/faq/ .
